## Summary

- **4 items claimed ✅ fixed were partially or fully verified:** B03 (ws_server disconnect) ✅ confirmed, B08 (version corner) ✅ confirmed.
- **4 items claimed ✅ fixed were ACTUALLY BUGGY:** B01 (UTF-8 centering) still broken, B05 (OLED config status) not implemented, B04/B06 (multi-ESP32 identification) only server-side half-done, B02 (frame animation) not implemented.
- **7 undocumented bugs discovered:** Primarily fragile string-parsing, UTF-8 issues still lingering despite claimed fix, dual WiFi manager confusion, and minor robustness concerns.
